## Phase 1: Foundation Enhancement

*Current through Q3 2026*

### Live Chat Support

Real-time security support built directly into the platform. When a business owner sees something suspicious at 2 AM, they shouldn't have to wait until morning for help. Our live chat system will connect users with security analysts who can provide immediate guidance.

- In-platform messaging with support ticket escalation
- Priority routing for Pro, Business, and Unlimited subscribers
- Chat history preserved for compliance documentation

### SMS & Mobile Threat Alerts

Critical threats don't wait for you to check your email. Our SMS alerting system will push high-priority notifications directly to your phone — ransomware activity targeting your industry, critical CVEs affecting your stack, or watchlist matches that need immediate attention.

- Configurable alert thresholds by severity
- Industry-specific threat notifications
- Available for Business and Unlimited tier subscribers

### Modular Add-On Marketplace

Not every business needs every feature. Our add-on system will let customers customize their STBCS experience by purchasing individual capabilities on top of their base subscription.

- Additional threat feed packs (industry-specific, regional)
- Premium tool access (advanced forensic analysis, deep OSINT)
- Extended data retention and historical analytics
- Custom report templates and white-label options

### Enhanced Email Intelligence

Expanding our email header analyzer into a full email security suite — phishing simulation, DMARC/DKIM/SPF monitoring, and business email compromise (BEC) detection.

- Email authentication health monitoring
- Phishing simulation campaigns for employee training
- Real-time BEC pattern detection
- Monthly email security posture reports

### Advanced Search & Filtering

Power users and security analysts need granular control. We're building advanced search with boolean operators, date ranges, feed-specific filtering, and saved search workflows that trigger automated actions.

- Complex query builder with AND/OR/NOT logic
- Cross-feed correlation searches
- Scheduled searches with alert triggers
- Exportable search results (CSV, JSON, STIX)

## Phase 2: Automation & Intelligence

*Q3 2026 through Q1 2027*

### AI-Powered Threat Scoring

Moving beyond static CVSS scores to dynamic, context-aware threat prioritization. Our AI engine will analyze your specific environment, industry, and technology stack to tell you which threats actually matter to *your* business — not just which ones have the highest generic score.

- Organization-specific risk scoring
- Technology stack mapping for vulnerability relevance
- Industry-vertical threat weighting
- Predicted exploit timeline estimation

### Automated Incident Response Playbooks

When a threat is detected, every minute counts. Pre-built and customizable playbooks will guide your team through response steps automatically — or execute them without human intervention for known threat patterns.

- Pre-built playbooks for ransomware, phishing, DDoS, and insider threats
- Custom playbook builder with drag-and-drop workflow
- Automated containment actions (IP blocking, account suspension recommendations)
- Post-incident report generation

### Predictive Threat Analytics

Using historical data from our 134+ feeds to identify emerging threat patterns before they become active campaigns. This shifts security from reactive to proactive — knowing what's coming, not just what's already here.

- Trend analysis across ransomware groups and attack vectors
- Seasonal and cyclical attack pattern identification
- Early warning indicators for emerging campaigns
- Monthly predictive threat briefings for subscribers

### SIEM & SOC Integration Layer

For businesses that already have security tools in place, STBCS will offer native integrations with popular SIEM platforms and SOC workflows — feeding our intelligence directly into their existing security operations.

- Syslog and CEF output for SIEM ingestion
- Splunk, Elastic, and Microsoft Sentinel integrations
- Webhook-based alert forwarding with custom payloads
- Bi-directional sync for case management

### Compliance Automation

Mapping security activities to compliance frameworks shouldn't require a consultant. We're building automated compliance tracking that maps your STBCS usage and security posture directly to NIST CSF, CIS Controls, ISO 27001, SOC 2, and HIPAA requirements.

- Automated evidence collection from platform activity
- Gap analysis with remediation recommendations
- Audit-ready report generation
- Continuous compliance monitoring dashboards

*Q1 2027 through Q4 2027*

For businesses that need more than a platform — they need a team. STBCS MDR will provide 24/7 human-led monitoring, threat hunting, and incident response powered by our platform and delivered by our experienced analysts.

- 24/7 threat monitoring and triage
- Proactive threat hunting campaigns
- Incident response with guaranteed SLAs
- Monthly security posture reviews
- Dedicated security analyst assignment for Enterprise clients

### Partner & MSSP Program

Managed Service Providers and IT consultancies serve thousands of SMBs. Our partner program will let them offer STBCS capabilities under their own brand, with multi-tenant management, consolidated billing, and volume pricing.

- White-label platform deployment
- Multi-tenant dashboard for managing client environments
- Consolidated billing and revenue sharing
- Partner certification and training program
- Co-marketing resources and deal registration

### Security Awareness Training Platform

The biggest vulnerability in any organization is its people. Our training platform will deliver ongoing, role-based cybersecurity education with simulated attacks and measurable outcomes.

- Role-based training paths (executive, technical, general staff)
- Simulated phishing campaigns with tracking
- Compliance-specific modules (HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOX)
- Gamified learning with progress dashboards
- Quarterly security culture assessments

### Attack Surface Management (ASM) Expansion

Building on our existing attack surface scanning, the expanded ASM module will provide continuous external asset discovery, shadow IT detection, and risk-ranked exposure management.

- Continuous asset discovery across domains, IPs, and cloud services
- Shadow IT and unknown asset detection
- Certificate monitoring and expiration alerting
- Third-party risk scoring for vendor management
- Historical exposure tracking and trend analysis

## Phase 4: Industry Leadership

*2028 and Beyond*

### Vertical-Specific Intelligence Packages

Cybersecurity isn't one-size-fits-all. A healthcare clinic faces different threats than a law firm or a manufacturing plant. We're developing industry-specific intelligence packages that deliver the most relevant threats, compliance mapping, and response playbooks for each sector.

- **Healthcare:** HIPAA-focused, medical device vulnerabilities, patient data protection
- **Financial Services:** PCI-DSS alignment, banking trojan tracking, wire fraud detection
- **Legal:** Client confidentiality protection, intellectual property monitoring, legal hold compliance
- **Manufacturing & ICS:** OT/ICS vulnerability tracking, supply chain risk monitoring
- **Retail & E-Commerce:** Payment fraud detection, Magecart tracking, POS security
- **Education:** Student data protection, FERPA compliance, research IP monitoring

### Global Threat Intelligence Network

Expanding our feed coverage and establishing regional intelligence partnerships to provide localized threat data for businesses operating across multiple geographies.

- Regional threat feed partnerships (EMEA, APAC, LATAM)
- Multi-language threat intelligence and alerts
- Geopolitical risk indicators for business operations
- Regional regulatory compliance mapping

### STBCS API Ecosystem

Opening our platform data to the broader security ecosystem. Developers and security teams will be able to build custom integrations, dashboards, and workflows on top of STBCS intelligence through a comprehensive API marketplace.

- Expanded REST API with full feed access
- GraphQL endpoint for flexible data queries
- SDKs for Python, JavaScript, Go, and PowerShell
- Community-contributed integrations and plugins
- Developer documentation portal and sandbox environment

### Cyber Insurance Integration

Partnering with cyber insurance carriers to provide policyholders with discounted STBCS access, and using platform data to help underwriters assess risk more accurately. Businesses using STBCS will benefit from lower premiums based on demonstrated security posture.

- Insurance carrier partnerships for bundled offerings
- Risk posture scoring accepted by partner insurers
- Automated evidence collection for claims support
- Premium discount qualification through platform usage metrics
