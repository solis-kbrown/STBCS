​Framework: Next.js (React) for a seamless, SEO-friendly frontend and robust API routes.
​Database: PostgreSQL (using an ORM like Prisma or Drizzle) to handle structured relationships between users, posts, comments, and tags.

​A modern, dark-mode, professional interface consistent with a tactical cybersecurity brand.
​Clear visual distinction between 'Official STB Documentation' and 'Community Contributions'.
​Badges or flair next to user names to indicate their trust level (e.g., 'STB Admin', 'Trusted Contributor', 'Member').

​Strategic Next Steps to Consider
​The "Trust" Algorithm: How will a user become "Trusted"? Will it be a manual promotion by you and your team, or do you want an automated system (e.g., after a user's account is active for 6 months and they receive 50 upvotes on their comments)? Starting with manual promotion is much safer and easier to build initially.
​Content Moderation: For a cybersecurity community, users will likely post code snippets, logs, and technical data. Replit will need to ensure the Markdown editor strips out malicious executable scripts while allowing safe formatting.
​Seamless Navigation: You'll want this to feel like a natural extension of stbcybersecurity.com. You might host this on a subdomain like docs.stbcybersecurity.com or community.stbcybersecurity.com.
